# vision copy

One file the founder edits. The app, the landing page, and the store listing all read their opening words from here. Everything is lowercase, and the promise is five minutes a day.

## opening screen

**headline:** knowing stuff is sexy

**two small lines:**
- make me cool
- become disgustingly cool

**lockup line (under the logo):** from the home of the abstract room

**tagline for the web and the store:** a daily workout for your personality

**the promise (screen two):** five minutes a day. one interesting thing. something to actually say at the houseparty.

## read more (the manifesto)

we've stopped being curious.

about concepts, ideas, cinema, literature, philosophy, music, society. somewhere between the job, the deadlines and "just one more reel," it all got quietly pushed aside. nobody decided it. it just happened.

so before the brain completely rots, let's spare five minutes a day for a small mental workout.

because let's be honest, nobody wants to be the one at the party who just nods along when the conversation gets good. you'd rather be the one with a take, a story, a reference nobody saw coming. the one people actually want to hang out with.

basically, cool.

## community line (welcome screen, about section)
