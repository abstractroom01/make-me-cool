# the abstract character: brief

## who

abstract is the mascot of the app and the same personality the community already knows from gatherings and messages. a slightly pretentious, culture-obsessed creature that thinks it knows better than everyone, and knows everyone is laughing at it. dry and showy on the surface, warm underneath.

## look

- a small, expressive creature built from the logo's overlapping squares, in teal and yellow.
- big expressive eyes, simple limbs, a body that can stretch and squash.
- clearly its own thing: not an owl, not an animal.
- flat shapes in brand colours only; no gradients that fight the brand.

## personality on screen

- playful judgement, mock superiority, dramatic announcements.
- never mean. the arrogance is a performance and the user is in on it.
- a soft side that appears at milestones, then snaps back ("okay, enough feelings").
- lines are written in the abstract voice (see the voice guide) and kept short.

## poses and animations (the set to commission)

1. **welcome:** waves from behind the headline on the first-run screens.
2. **thinking:** chin on a limb, used on the comparison screen and while content loads.
3. **cheer:** the right-answer reaction and the result screen.
4. **shrug:** the wrong-answer reaction, said kindly.
5. **dance:** completion celebration, with confetti.
6. **spark:** lights the streak spark and grows with it.
7. **rest:** sleeping or lounging, for weekends and empty states.

## where it appears

first-run screens (where it speaks in speech bubbles, so the art needs room for them), quiz feedback sheets, the result screen, milestone screens, the spark moment, empty states, and the about section. share cards carry only the abstract room mark, not the character.

## production plan

- build the app with simple animated placeholder shapes and the same file names and sizes as the final art.
- the founder commissions an illustrator or animator for the seven poses before launch; final files replace the placeholders with no code changes.
- deliver as animation files the app can play, plus still frames for reduced motion.

## to decide later

- exact colour proportions of teal and yellow on the body.
- an outfit or colour set that gems can unlock (see the gems requirement).
