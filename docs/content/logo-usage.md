# logo usage

All files are in `assets/brand/` (transparent backgrounds). The untouched originals are in `assets/brand/originals/`.

## files

| file | what it is | use it on |
|---|---|---|
| `logo-main.png` | multicolour logo with the wordmark: teal gradient squares, yellow outline, white text | deep teal #04363A, brand teal #098B93, or black. This is the main logo |
| `logo-teal.png` | one-colour teal logo with the wordmark | off-white and white screens, the quiet screens (quiz review, settings) |
| `logo-white.png` | one-colour white logo with the wordmark | yellow, red, and photo backgrounds where white reads well, and share cards on dark |
| `logo-black.png` | one-colour black logo with the wordmark | yellow and cream backgrounds where black reads best |
| `icon-main.png`, `icon-teal.png`, `icon-white.png`, `icon-black.png` | the overlapping-squares icon alone | app icon, small spaces, and the character's body |

## rules

- the multicolour logo is the main logo. the one-colour versions are alternates.
- the main logo's wordmark is white, so it only works on dark backgrounds. on light backgrounds use the teal version.
- keep clear space on all sides (the exported files already include it); never stretch, rotate, or recolour.
- the app icon is the icon alone on a deep teal square, 1024 by 1024 with no transparency; Android also needs a foreground and a background layer.

## open point for the founder

the yellow in the supplied multicolour logo is #FCED0A, which the brand guide lists as a retired colour. the app's own yellow is #FFCC00. the logo files are kept exactly as supplied. decide whether the logo keeps its yellow as the one exception, or whether a version in #FFCC00 should be made.
