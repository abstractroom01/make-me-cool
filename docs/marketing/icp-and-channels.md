# ICPs and channels for makemecool.me

Research date: 29 Oct 2026. Method: customer-research, "digital watering hole" mode, with proxy evidence because there are no reviews or interviews of make me cool yet. **Every persona below is provisional**; each is tagged with its proxy source and a confidence level (high / medium / low). Replace them with first-party evidence after the first 50 feedback answers.

## What we know and don't know

- **Known:** the product is free, mobile first, five minutes, and it has a built-in share loop. The Abstract Room is an existing community with a real audience and a real person at its head.
- **Unknown, and it decides the plan:** how many people the Abstract Room can reach directly today (WhatsApp community and broadcast lists, Instagram followers, newsletter, event attendee list). The website says "80,000+ community". Public pages from Ashoka University give about 2,500 to 3,100 members from earlier years. These cannot both be today's number. **Day 0 of the plan asks the founder for the real figures.**

## ICPs (ranked by how likely they are to play and share in 10 days)

### 1. The abstract room's own members and followers (confidence: high)
- **Who:** people who already attend film, book and ideas events, online or offline.
- **Why first:** they already trust the founder and the brand, they want "more of it between events", and they need no explaining. The ten-day goal rises or falls on how many of these we reach.
- **Proxy source:** the community itself (six years, 700+ gatherings) and Ashoka's coverage of the founder.
- **Reach:** WhatsApp community, Instagram, email list, ambassadors and admins, offline events.

### 2. The founder's personal network (confidence: high)
- **Who:** friends, ex-classmates and colleagues (Ashoka Young India Fellowship alumni, Masters' Union, college and school groups), roughly 24 to 35, working.
- **Why:** they match the target age, it costs one message, and they will give honest feedback.
- **Reach:** personal WhatsApp contacts, LinkedIn, alumni groups. Needs the founder's own accounts.

### 3. Pop-culture nerds and quizzers (confidence: medium)
- **Who:** people who love trivia, cinema, music and books; campus quiz clubs and alumni who quiz.
- **Proxy evidence:** India has a large campus quizzing circuit. Institutes such as IIM Indore, IIM Kashipur and IIT Kanpur run quiz clubs; pop culture is a stated focus of clubs like Manipal Jaipur's QUREKA and of fest events (Gargi College's "Pop Quiz, But OK"); there are national events and cross-institute festivals. These audiences already share trivia for fun.
- **Caveat:** many are students (18 to 24), so they overlap only partly with the 23+ target. Good for early sharing, not for the core.
- **Reach:** DM to club handles or presidents; alumni quiz groups. Low time cost if you send one templated message.

### 4. The "daily 5-minute habit" crowd (confidence: medium)
- **Who:** readers of daily short newsletters (finshots is the closest example) and wordle-style players.
- **Proxy evidence:**
  - Finshots grew from a college project to a large daily audience without paid ads, by making something small, daily and easy to share, though specific tactics are not documented in what we found.
  - Wordle's growth is widely attributed to its share grid: a result you can post without spoiling anything, which doubles as an ad. Our share card (result squares, day number, the line) is built on the same idea.
- **Reach:** small India-focused newsletters and creators for swaps (second week; takes more time).

### 5. Early-career professionals on LinkedIn (confidence: low to medium)
- **Who:** 23 to 30, ambitious, want to sound sharp in meetings.
- **Reach:** a founder post about building it, plus "the story behind the story" posts. Mixed ICP fit, but cheap and it adds credibility.

### 6. Maker communities (confidence: high for feedback, low for core ICP)
- **Who:** indie makers (Peerlist, developer subreddits, the Shipped.money cohort).
- **Value:** quick first plays, honest critique, credibility. They are not the target user.
- **Rules found:** r/developersIndia allows apps and sites under an "I Made This" flair and a monthly showcase thread, and bans self-promotion that is not a project; r/India does not allow promotional posts or advertisements, so do not post there. Check each sidebar and wiki before posting.

## Channels, ranked (zero budget, 15 minutes a day)

| Rank | Channel | Why | Founder time | Expected role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Abstract Room WhatsApp community and broadcast | warm, trusts you, one tap to open | 5 min on 4 days | **the engine.** most of the 1,000 |
| 2 | Instagram (story with link sticker, bio link, one reel) | the abstract room's home channel | 5 to 10 min on 4 days | steady trickle plus social proof |
| 3 | Founder's personal WhatsApp and status | warm, matches the age band | 3 min a day | first 100 and a feedback loop |
| 4 | The product's own share loop (card, link, "N people played from your link") | free, runs while you sleep | 0 | multiplier on every channel above |
| 5 | Alumni and cohort groups (Ashoka YIF, Masters' Union, Shipped.money) | trusted, relevant | 5 min once or twice | a few hundred plays |
| 6 | LinkedIn founder posts (2 posts) | credibility and reach to professionals | 8 min twice | 50 to 200 plays |
| 7 | Campus quiz clubs and micro-newsletters (outreach) | slow, but compounds | 10 min on 1 to 2 days | long-tail |
| 8 | Reddit | rules are strict; only a maker subreddit with the "I Made This" flair is safe | skip in the first 10 days | skip |

**Skip for now:** Product Hunt (launch day starts at 1:30 pm India time, and a typical indie launch brings a few hundred visitors, mostly non-ICP), Hacker News, paid ads, SEO (too slow), cold email.

## Honest funnel math (planning assumptions, not measurements)

A "user" in this plan means a **person who starts at least one story**. No sign-up is needed, so that is the number we can measure.

For a warm WhatsApp or Instagram audience, a reasonable planning range is that about 5% to 15% of those reached open a link, and about 50% to 70% of the people who land go on to start a story, because there is no sign-up wall. These are assumptions to be replaced by the first two days of real data.

| If the abstract room can reach directly… | Expected plays from owned channels (10 days) | Plus personal network, alumni, LinkedIn, share loop | Likely total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8,000 or more people | 500 to 1,200 | 250 to 500 | **1,000 is likely** |
| about 3,000 people | 200 to 500 | 250 to 500 | 450 to 1,000, **possible, not sure** |
| about 1,000 people | 70 to 170 | 250 to 500 | 320 to 670, **short of 1,000** |

If we fall short, the fix is not more posting; it is a bigger reachable audience (more groups, partners, a creator) in days 6 to 10.
