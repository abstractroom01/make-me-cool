# make me cool: 10-day, zero-budget launch plan (1,000 users, 15 minutes a day)

Prepared 29 Oct 2026. Built from `.agents/product-marketing.md` (the positioning kit) and `docs/marketing/icp-and-channels.md` (ICPs, channels, funnel math). Copy to paste is in `docs/marketing/launch-assets.md`.

This is a compact adaptation of a full AARRR plan: ten days, one founder, fifteen minutes a day. Every action is tagged with its AARRR stage. Everything that can be prepared in advance has been, so on each day you copy, paste and look at one number.

## 1. The goal and what counts

- **Goal:** 1,000 users in 10 days, no paid spend.
- **A user is** a person who starts at least one story (no sign-up needed). This is measured, not guessed.
- **Quality guard:** at least 35% of those people finish the story, and at least 15% come back on another day. A thousand one-time visitors is a spike; it is not a habit.
- **Sign-ups are not the goal.** Signing in is a bonus (it saves the streak and starts the Plus trial). Money is a later step. Do not push ₹99 in these ten days.

## 2. The launch gate: content (read this first)

The app has stories through **30 October** only. After that it shows the last story as "latest". A daily product with no new story the next morning breaks its own promise, and it wastes the first-day visitors.

**Do not start Day 1 until there is a buffer of at least 10 stories** (31 Oct to 9 Nov). Batch 2 of the story bank has 18 researched candidates (`docs/content/story-bank-batch-2.md`). Claude writes the stories; you read and approve.

Proposed calendar: **Day 0 is today, Day 1 is Monday 2 Nov** (weekday mornings suit working professionals on WhatsApp). That needs stories written for 31 Oct to 11 Nov, about 12 in all.

## 3. Funnel math (assumptions, replace with real data by Day 3)

The result depends on one number you have to give us on Day 0: **how many people can the Abstract Room reach directly?** See the table in `icp-and-channels.md`. In short: with 8,000 or more reachable people, 1,000 is likely; with about 3,000, it is possible; with about 1,000 it is not, and Days 6 to 10 must widen the audience.

## 6. Tracking and the daily number check

- **Links:** every channel gets its own tag, for example `https://makemecool.me/?utm_source=wa&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=d2`. The app now records the source on every landing. A full list is in the assets file.
- **Four numbers every day:** (1) landing views, (2) users (started a story), (3) finish rate (finished / started), (4) shares (`share_tapped`). Plus the best source.
- **Where:** Claude reads them from the database on request ("numbers"). Microsoft Clarity shows recordings of real visits (data can lag by up to 2 hours).
- **Not measured yet:** day-7 return rate; it needs 7 days of data, so it is the number to read on Day 10.

## 7. Rules for double down and stop

- **After Day 3:** work out plays per minute of effort for each channel. Do more of the top two. Drop the bottom one.
- **If a post gets under 2% of the people reached to play:** the copy is the problem. Change the first line, not the channel.
- **If finish rate is under 25%:** the story is too long or unclear. Tell Claude which story, and it will fix the content.
- **If more than 1 in 5 people say "i don't get it":** stop promoting and fix the landing page first.

## 8. What Claude does so you don't have to

- Writes the 12 stories, with sources, quiz and share lines (you read and approve).
- Drafts every post, message and reel script (done: see assets).
- Adds tracking, pulls the numbers, and fixes anything that breaks.
- **Cannot** post, send messages or open your accounts. Everything outward-facing is copied by you.

1. **How many people can the abstract room reach directly** (WhatsApp, Instagram, email)? This single number decides if 1,000 is likely.
2. **Day 1 date:** start Monday 2 Nov, once the stories are ready?
3. **Who else can share?** Two or three abstract room admins or ambassadors who will repost on Day 2 would double the reach.
4. **Payments:** test keys are live; no push for Plus in these 10 days. Confirm.
5. **The "80,000+" number:** confirm it is current before it appears in any message.
