# 09 · Testing the stories with 5 real people

Goal: find out if a stranger can read a story, understand it, and retell it. About 15 minutes each. You watch; you do not help.

## Who (5 people)

Pick people who fit the target and do not know the app. Mix them:
1. 23–27, early career
2. 28–35, busy professional
3. 36+, skeptical about apps
4. someone whose first language is not English
5. someone who says they are "not a culture person"

No friends who will be polite. No one from the team.

## Setup

- Their own phone. Open https://makemecool.me for them, or send the link.
- Give each person a different story if you can (use the "earlier stories" list). Note which.
- Ask to screen-record or sit beside them. Say nothing while they read.
- Tell them: "You can't get anything wrong here. I'm testing the app, not you. Say out loud whatever you're thinking."

## Script

**Before (1 min)**
- "What did you do the last time you learned something interesting? Where did it come from?"

**Task (7 min, think aloud, no help)**
- "Open the app and do today's story. Say what you think as you go."
- Note: every pause, every re-read, every "what does that mean?", every tap you didn't expect.
- Do not explain. If they ask a question, write it down and say "what would you guess?"

**Retell (2 min)**
- Close the phone. "Tell me the story as if I'd never heard it."
- Then: "What would you say to a friend about it?"

**After (4 min)**
- "Was anything confusing or a word you didn't know?"
- "Where did you want to stop?"
- "Did you tap 'go deeper'? Why or why not? Which of those would you actually try?"
- "Did you like it? Would you open this tomorrow? What would make you forget?"
- "Anything that felt wrong or untrue?"

## How to read the results

- **Retell failed** (3 or more of 5 cannot say the core idea): the story is the problem. Rewrite it.
- **Same word confuses 2+ people:** explain it in the card where it first appears.
- **Quiz felt like a test** to 2+ people: soften wording or add more "show me the answer".
- **Nobody taps go deeper:** make the top pick more visible, or put one item on the result card.
- **Fewer than 3 of 5 say they would open it tomorrow:** the daily loop is the risk, not the writing. Dig into why.

Do not average the scores. Five people find problems; they do not prove what works.

## After the sessions

Write the top 5 fixes, make them, and test again with 3 new people. Also read the in-app thumbs and "spot a mistake" notes (table `story_feedback`) alongside this.

## A form for people who can't meet

Send them the link plus these questions (any form tool, nothing personal collected):
1. Which story did you read? (pick)
2. In one or two sentences, what was it about? (their words, not ours)
3. Which words or names did you not know?
4. Did you finish the quiz? Was any question unfair?
5. Did you try "go deeper"? What looked interesting?
6. How much did you like it? (1–5)
7. Would you open this tomorrow? (yes / maybe / no) Why?
8. Anything that seemed wrong or confusing?
