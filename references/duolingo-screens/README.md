# Duolingo reference screens

Twenty screenshots from the founder (ten saved as files, ten described below) (Duolingo onboarding and lesson flow), kept as design references. Nothing is copied from them; they show patterns to adapt.

## What they show

- **Conversational first run.** A character introduces itself ("Hi there! I'm Duo!"), then asks questions in a speech bubble with a progress bar across the top and one large choice per row.
- **Daily goal question.** Four options (3, 10, 15, 30 minutes a day), each labelled casual, regular, serious, or intense.
- **Value promise before the first lesson.** "Here's what you can achieve in 3 months!" with three benefits, then "Here's your first 2 minute lesson."
- **In-lesson feedback.** A bottom sheet after each answer: green "Nicely done!" with a CONTINUE button; "Bye-bye mistake!" when a previously missed item is answered correctly, with a "previous mistake" tag.
- **Momentum counters.** A "COMBO x5" and "COMBO x12" label above a progress bar that turns orange.
- **Milestone moment.** A full-screen "10 in a row!" with an animated character.
- **Completion screen.** "You're a learner! I'm proud of you already!!" with three stat cards (total XP, accuracy, time), a CLAIM XP button, and a share button.

## Second batch (described here; image files not copied)

- **Streak ignition.** On the first completed day a dark screen asks the user to "Swipe up to ignite your streak!" over a glowing flame.
- **Streak screens.** "A streak is born!" with a big day count and a week strip of seven dots with the finished day ticked; then "Reach a Streak Goal to evolve your flame!" with goals of 7, 14, 30, and 50 days, each with a reward, and a "Commit to my goal" button.
- **Daily quests.** "All Daily Quests complete!" with a progress bar ending in a treasure chest.
- **Home.** A top bar with course, streak, gems, and energy; a path of lesson circles; a bottom bar of six tabs.
- **Profile.** A handle with join year, courses, following and followers counts, an Add Friends button with a QR code, and a "Finish your profile! 2 steps left" card; below it an overview (streak, XP), monthly badges, and achievements with levels.
- **Combo result.** "You started a combo! 12 right answers in a row? Keep it up!" with stat cards and a share button.

## How they map to this plan

- Adopted: celebrations, in-quiz feedback, milestone moments, a character-led first run, a growing streak (as a spark rather than a flame), a week strip, streak goals, daily quests with a chest, gems, badges and achievements, QR invites, and a "finish your profile" card (R20, R21, R29, R31 to R34).
- Not adopted: a combo counter (deferred), followers and following, the path-map home, and the daily time goal.
