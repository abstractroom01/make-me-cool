# Headway reference screens

Fifteen screenshots from the founder (nine saved as files, six described below) showing Headway's onboarding, home, streak, plans, and short-form feed. Kept as design references only; claims shown in them (for example "5x more likely to reach set goals" or "55M+ users") are Headway's own marketing and are not repeated in this product.

## What they show

- **Value comparison.** "Typical book: 300+ pages, 8 to 10 hours, read only" next to "Book summary: key ideas, 15 minutes, read and listen", then a one-line promise.
- **Catalogue breadth.** A grid of book covers and topic chips with "2000+ summaries always at hand".
- **Daily time question.** Four options with what each adds up to, and "You can always change it later", with a friendly character.
- **Commit to a streak.** 7, 14, 30, and 50 day goals, each with a label, and a "Commit and continue" button.
- **Notification set-up framed around a moment.** "Create a habit of growing during lunchtime", a sample notification, and toggles for learning reminders, motivational pushes, and fresh picks.
- **Personalising screen.** A progress bar that fills while short yes or no questions appear, with social proof at the bottom.
- **Insight card.** A full-screen coloured card with a story-style segmented progress bar, one sentence, Share and Remember buttons, and a link to the source.
- **Home.** Themed rows of summaries and a "Roll the dice" button for a random pick; a bottom bar of Home, Plans, Shorts, Search, and Library.
- **Streak screen.** A week strip M to S with "Finish 3 key points every day to extend your streak. Miss a day and it resets to 0."
- **Growth plans.** A list of themed plans, and a plan view with "You'll master" outcomes and steps that unlock in order.
- **Shorts and "Did you know?"** A vertical feed of one-line insights with like and save buttons and a Saved tab.

## How they map to this plan

- Adopted: the value comparison, the streak goal commitment, notification toggles by type, Remember and Share on takeaway cards, the cool lines feed with a Saved tab, and a library with a random pick (R7, R20, R31, R35 to R37).
- Not adopted: the daily time question, the personalising screen, and growth plans (themed weeks wait for v2).
