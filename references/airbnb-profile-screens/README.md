# Airbnb profile-building reference screens

Seven screenshots from the founder showing Airbnb's step-by-step, optional profile flow. Kept as design references only.

## What they show

- **A list of playful prompts.** "What's a fun fact about you?", "What are you obsessed with?", "What do you spend too much time doing?", "What would your biography title be?", "What's your most useless skill?", "Where did you go to school?", "Where have you always wanted to travel?", "What do you do for work?", "Do you have any pets?". Each is a card with an icon and a fill-in-the-blank stem.
- **Optional, but encouraged.** "Answer at least one question about you. These are optional, but we recommend answering a few."
- **Interest chips.** "What are you into?" with tappable chips and a "0/20 selected" counter.
- **A short intro.** "Introduce yourself in a sentence or two", optional, with a placeholder that starts the sentence for the user.
- **A visibility choice.** "Do you want to show your travel history?" with a toggle, so each kind of detail has its own privacy control.
- **A photo step.** "Add a profile photo", with a coloured initial as the default avatar.
- **Frame.** A segmented progress bar at the bottom, an Exit button at the top, and Back and Next buttons.

## How they map to this plan

They shape the optional profile in the plan: playful prompts instead of a form, optional by default, interest chips, a per-detail visibility control, and an initial-letter avatar.
